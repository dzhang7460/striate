How Striate Works

Simple, evidence-based guidance for beginners without chatbot fluff.

What Readiness Means

0 – 10 scale

Readiness is a simple composite score combining your sleep duration, energy, muscle soreness, and mood.

  • 8.0 – 10.0: Strong recovery. Great day for a full workout session.
  • 5.0 – 7.9: Moderate recovery. Good for consistent training at normal effort.
  • Below 5.0: Depleted recovery. Striate will reduce volume or prescribe active recovery.

What Confidence Means

We never pretend to be 100% sure when data is limited. Confidence levels show how certain the recommendation is:

High confidence

Clear, consistent inputs pointing to a well-studied beginner routine or rest day.

Medium confidence

Mixed signals (e.g., moderate soreness or limited check-in history) — a sensible middle path.

Low confidence

Pain, injury, or unusual constraints flagged. Defaults to safety first.

How the AI Shortens or Moves Workouts

Striate is a schedule-aware decision engine. It adjusts your daily schedule around three main rules:

  • Shortening sessions: When you report ≤15 minutes or a busy day, it shortens your session to a 10-minute minimum to protect the habit without zeroing out.
  • Moving to recovery: When short sleep (<6h) combines with low energy, it moves lifting to tomorrow and prescribes an easy 15–20 min outdoor walk.
  • Adapting to completion: If yesterday was completed, it maintains your momentum. If skipped, it asks why and simplifies starting today.

If Something Feels Wrong

Muscle soreness is normal; sharp or joint pain is not. Never train through sharp, worsening, or joint pain. If you experience pain, flag "Pain / Injury" on your check-in so Striate stops training that area. Always consult a medical professional for injuries — an app cannot diagnose you.
